SENSE → SAVE → RECOVER

ZERO SHELF

Zero Waste. Smarter Shelves. Fresher Future.

SENSE – simulated RFID tags and shelf sensors track every product and its expiry.
SAVE – discounts rise automatically as expiry nears (10% / 20% / 30%).
RECOVER – expired food is blocked from sale and routed to composting, biogas or other recovery.
Everything here is a software simulation – no real RFID hardware or payments.
